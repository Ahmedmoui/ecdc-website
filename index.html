Why an Engineer-Led Builder

One firm accountable for the design and the build

Most projects hand off between an engineer, an architect, and a contractor — and problems fall through the cracks in between. At ECDC, a licensed Professional Engineer designs, engineers, and builds your project, so the details are right on paper and in the field.

One Accountable Expert

Design, structural engineering, and construction come from a single firm led by a Ph.D. Professional Engineer. That means fewer handoffs, tighter drawings, and one point of responsibility from first sketch to final walkthrough.

Built for the Gulf Coast

We engineer to the Florida Panhandle's high-velocity wind loads and detail continuous load paths from roof to foundation. Insulated Concrete Form (ICF) walls, engineered connections, and impact-rated openings are part of how we build — not upgrades we hope you'll skip.

Energy Conscious by Design

Lower utility bills start with the design, not the thermostat. We plan solar orientation, high-performance insulation and glazing, and efficient envelopes so your building stays comfortable and costs less to run for decades.
