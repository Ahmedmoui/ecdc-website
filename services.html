Civil & Site-Plan Design

Design & Engineering

Every successful project starts with drawings that hold up — to the permit office and to the realities of the site. As a Ph.D. Professional Engineer, our principal prepares and seals the civil and structural documents your project needs, then builds from them, so the intent never gets lost in translation.

We handle the unglamorous but critical work that keeps a project on schedule: parking and circulation layouts, utility routing, stormwater and drainage design, grading, and the permit-ready drawing sets that local reviewers expect. Because we also build, our plans are practical to construct, not just correct on paper.

  • Site plans, parking & circulation design
  • Utility, grading & stormwater / drainage plans
  • Structural engineering & sealed permit drawings
  • Wind-load & load-path engineering for the Panhandle
  • Plan review, permitting & agency coordination

Design-Build Residential

Custom Homes & Major Remodels

Your home should fit your life and your lot — and stand up to the coast. We design and build custom residences from the ground up, engineering the structure for local wind zones while tailoring the layout, light, and finishes to how you actually live.

Renovating instead? Major remodels are where an engineer-builder earns their keep. When we open up walls and find the unexpected, we can design the fix on the spot instead of stopping to wait on an outside engineer.

  • Design-build custom homes, coast to inland lots
  • Whole-home & structural remodels

Efficiency Engineered In

Energy-Conscious Systems

"Energy conscious" is in our name because it belongs in the design, not bolted on at the end. We orient buildings to work with the sun, specify high-performance insulation and glazing, and build tight, durable envelopes that hold their conditioned air — so your systems run less and last longer.

Where it makes sense, we build with Insulated Concrete Forms (ICF) for walls that are quiet, storm-resistant, and highly insulated in one system, and we design in readiness for geothermal, solar, and rainwater harvesting so future upgrades are simple.

  • ICF (Insulated Concrete Form) construction
  • Passive & active solar orientation
  • Geothermal-ready mechanical design
  • High-performance glazing & insulation
  • Rainwater harvesting & efficient envelopes
